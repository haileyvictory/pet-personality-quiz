Privacy & credits

Your interview stays with you

Your pet's optional name and answers stay in page memory. No accounts, uploads, analytics, cookies, browser storage, or AI service calls. Reloading clears the interview.

GitHub Pages receives normal hosting requests and network metadata, including IP addresses. Read GitHub's privacy statement for details. Following an external link takes you to that site's privacy practices.

A little credit where it's due

Blobby and the hat sprites are Microsoft Corporation artwork from the MIT-licensed VS Code project. The 24 career outfits combine those sprites with custom colors and original pixel-art job props. They are quiz illustrations, not actual VS Code achievement unlocks. The 48 cat and dog portraits are prepared AI-generated storybook illustrations, not uploaded pet photos. They include the six original box executive, snack inspector, and neighborhood lookout portraits.

This is an independent, unofficial project. It is not endorsed by Microsoft or GitHub, and it is not a real personality assessment.
